Make the result reproducible

Write down the city, Gregorian date, Asr convention and Isha option before investigating a result. Saying that a timetable is for Saudi Arabia does not identify its coordinates. A short record of the inputs lets another person repeat the calculation instead of guessing what your screen showed.

For more background on how prayer times are calculated, the awqat-alsala guide connects the timetable with its location, date and stated method. Read that explanation alongside your recorded inputs, then decide which variable deserves investigation. This gives a discrepancy a clearer question than two isolated clock readings, without treating the name of a calculation method as proof that every published table must agree.

First change only the city, then return to the original selection. Next try a different day without changing location. This separates two effects that would be harder to interpret if several fields moved together. The exercise explains the tool’s behaviour; it does not establish a religious ruling.

When asking about a discrepancy, include the relevant prayer and the size of the difference as well as those inputs. A cropped image of two clock readings leaves too much unknown. Identify the cause before adding a permanent correction to a personal alarm or a shared timetable.

Separate an instant from its clock display

Adhan calculates times as Date values, which are then formatted for a specified time zone. The same instant can have different clock labels in different countries. This guide displays results in Asia/Riyadh; it does not infer your prayer location from your computer’s own time zone.

The date selector supplies the Gregorian day to calculate. If you are browsing from abroad near midnight, check which Saudi date your plan concerns. Your device’s local calendar date and the date needed for a Saudi city may differ, even when both clocks are functioning correctly.

Read the Umm al-Qura and Asr choices precisely

The Umm al-Qura parameters use an 18.5° Fajr angle and a default Isha interval of 90 minutes after Maghrib. The Ramadan option changes that interval to 120 minutes. An automatic calendar-based choice or a manual setting is a calculation instruction, not a lunar sighting or confirmation of the month.

The Shafi and Hanafi labels select different Asr calculation conventions in the library. Keep the other inputs unchanged to see that setting’s effect. This explanation is not a fatwa; questions about the practice you should follow belong with the qualified religious authority you rely on.

A city coordinate does not describe every horizon

The reference point represents an approximate city location. It does not capture a nearby building or a ridge outside your window. The moment the sun disappears behind a local obstruction cannot simply be substituted for the astronomical sunset definition used by the calculation.

This model also leaves the horizon-elevation correction at its default rather than substituting altitude above sea level. Investigate meaningful differences with the method and a trusted local source. Minute-level display is convenient for reading, but it does not imply measurement of every local condition.

A useful answer

Questions that help you use the guide

Does “Umm al-Qura” mean an official timetable is being copied?

Here it names calculation parameters in Adhan. The tool does not copy mosque announcements or monitor an actual adhan, and it does not claim that every local authority uses these settings without adjustment.

Why can Asr change while Fajr and Maghrib stay the same?

The Asr selector changes the convention used for that prayer. Keep the city, date and other options fixed, then compare the row affected by the input you changed.

Does the Ramadan option determine when the month starts?

It controls the additional Isha interval. Even automatic mode uses a calendar calculation rather than a lunar observation. Follow the calendar or announcement you rely on for the month itself.

Should I add the city’s altitude to the calculation?

Altitude above sea level is different from height above the visible horizon. Without the appropriate horizon measurement, this reference calculation leaves the relevant correction at its default instead of guessing.

Method and references

How is the time calculated?

Times are calculated astronomically with Adhan JS using the stated settings. City coordinates come from GeoNames under CC BY 4.0 and represent approximate centres. We do not use elevation above sea level as elevation above the horizon. Consult the locally adopted timetable and mosque announcement when they differ.
